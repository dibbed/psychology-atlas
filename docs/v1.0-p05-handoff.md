## Source manager

- Routes: `/admin-console/sources`, `/sources/new`, `/sources/[id]`, retained history, plan detail and bibliography export. Search separates title from exact normalized DOI/PMID; URLs retain edition query parameters.
- Create, update, archive, unused delete, merge, split and successor all use a persisted `SourcePlan`. Preview has schema/version 1; apply creates a unique receipt and exposes version 2. Direct source mutation endpoints do not exist.
- Preview and apply share `_validate`, with fresh authority/session checks and lock order `policy_lock → lock_brain_curation → source rows`. Validation executes real per-object saves in a rolled-back transaction. Apply rechecks canonical data, claim ownership/membership/notes, archived references, identity competitors, policy, mappings and the stored fingerprint.
- Merge requires confirmed identity, conflict-free DOI/PMID and resource version, explicit choices for each differing field, and a complete consumer mapping. Split uses independently created, distinct targets and a complete mapping. No automatic deduplication, claim concatenation or source deletion occurs.
- Checked Assessment/Brain provenance stays protected. `protect_assessment_source` and `lock_brain_curation` remain active; changing the typed `resource_version`, moving a source link or using raw admin cannot bypass them. Successor lineage preserves the earlier claims and blocks cycles.
- Immutable review/governance/DSM/research references are retained and appear in impact/manual plans. Indirect Assessment findings bind their study source. Historical locators and FK identities remain in internal retained history.
- Source detail/plan/history requires all affected domain view permissions before exposing impact or counts. Moves also require the consuming domain's edit permission. Bibliography/export omit raw metadata. Raw Django SourceReference admin is read-only.
- The list does not scan impact: `usage_count` and `impact_available` are null until scoped detail is requested. On the upgraded real-data copy, serializing 30 bibliography rows changed from **16.711s / 2751 queries** to **0.011s / 30 queries**. These are local serialization measurements, not network latency or a production SLA. An API regression checks that pagination never scans private impact and remains below 80 total queries.

## Rights and terminology

`/admin-console/rights` and `/admin-console/terminology` share the existing session/CSRF/MFA shell and expose typed resource/evidence lookups, proposed revisions, reviewer assignment, decisions, reopen and immutable history. Rights also exposes explicit revoke and expired/overdue queues. Mutations after creation require a strong `If-Match`.

Rights scope is exactly **resource type + ID + version + material + intended use**. Resource content and declared parents have a separate digest, so stable edition keys cannot make altered content fresh. Evidence includes source IDs and locators; holder evidence is separate from ordinary evidence. Public access never implies permission. Unknown accepts missing holder/evidence/dates rather than inventing facts. An allowed proposal requires identified holder, evidence, dates and limitations; approval checks current dates and dependencies.

Workflow status and computed rights status are separate. Unknown, restricted, conflict, expired, revoked, stale, not effective and review overdue remain distinct. Expiry/review dates are inclusive; expiry/overdue occurs after the recorded day. Same-scope incompatible effective decisions deny as conflict. A permission for metadata/public display grants neither item access nor commercial use.

Only an immutable approve/revoke decision changes the effective public gate. Pending/rejected proposals grant no permission and cannot withdraw content. Reopen/edit preserves the earlier effective decision snapshot, still subject to live target, evidence, reviewer, policy and date checks; a later explicit decision replaces it. Old or stale approvals cannot silently become fresh.

P04's solo-owner acknowledgement and two-person exclusion of every contributor apply to these reviews. Scope denial returns 404 for foreign-domain records/history; queue filtering happens before counts/pagination. Services independently recheck current session, permissions, policy and reviewer conflict. An approval reason is `RIGHTS_SCOPE_VERIFIED` or `TERMINOLOGY_SELECTED`; it never grants scientific review.

Persian terminology records distinguish display name and alias and retain EN/FA, alternatives, source locators, selection note, reviewer and plain-text RTL preview. Candidate conflicts are bounded within the authorized domain. Approval changes no canonical name, Assessment form authorization, scientific status or canonical rights fields.

## Consuming boundaries

- P04 dependency hashes now bind source lifecycle/lineage and rights/terminology scopes, including an exact target whose legal evidence differs from its scientific sources. Source mutation, rights revocation, stale reviewer/policy and date changes invalidate the prior scientific decision without rewriting its history.
- Public list/detail/search/graph and nested serializers intersect effective metadata/public-display rights with existing active/scientific/public gates. No new reviewed flag or legacy backfill grants publication. Source lifecycle/rights hide public bibliography without changing canonical scientific resolution.
- Text and explicit translated prose are independently redacted. Assessment access uses its own resource/version/material/use; item/translation denial does not hide otherwise permitted metadata or authorize an unknown form.
- Quiz items and Flashcard front/back/hint are withheld for denied item scope. Public submission/review and Case start/resume/decision routes check metadata/item rights before emitting content or writing learning effects. Existing attempts, histories and SRS records are retained.
- The new real Case-start regression exposed an existing PostgreSQL nullable-join lock error. Start/resume/advance now lock the canonical Case/attempt rows with `select_for_update(of=("self",))`; optional read-only revision/step joins are excluded. Duplicate-start and duplicate-decision concurrency invariants remain covered.
- Public scope lookup is cached only within the current request/transaction and reads current SQL once per normal public request. A process-local stale cache cannot conceal another worker's revocation. Graph payloads with registered rights/lifecycle restrictions are re-evaluated; legacy graph caching and uncommitted-preview isolation remain. Query budgets include exactly this fixed scope read, with growth comparisons retained.

It adds `SourceState`, `SourcePlan`, `SourcePlanReceipt`, `SourceHistory`, `SourceSuccessor`, `GovernanceRecord`, `GovernanceRevision`, `GovernanceDecision`, their indexes/constraints and retained history relationships. It has no data migration, fabricated permission, scientific approval or publication backfill.

The original `backend/data/psychology_atlas.sqlite3` had applied Atlas head **0036** and was opened read-only for backup. Its copied database was upgraded through **0041**. All **123 existing Atlas tables** retained identical row counts and SHA256 data digests, integrity was `ok`, and foreign-key violations were zero. New governance records/decisions/plans/source histories were all zero. The original file SHA256 remained unchanged. The disposable migration test also checks `0040 → 0041 → 0040` without losing legacy rows or generating decisions.

Do not mistake the local copy upgrade for migration of the user's running database. Back up the selected deployment database and apply the project's complete pending migration chain through 0041 before using P05 there; P01 owner/MFA provisioning and production gates still apply.

## Limits and next dependencies

- Evidence uploads/downloads are not exposed. P05 accepts existing source/URL/locator references; private artifacts remain **P06**.
- Dynamic canonical publication, broader canonical editors, upload/intake jobs and release/deployment remain their planned later phases. P05 does not replace the pinned Brain/Assessment publishers or grant canonical Assessment translation authorization.
- P05 fixture evidence is visibly synthetic/non-scientific, uses reserved `.invalid` URLs and disposable databases. Reviewed fixture flags exercise existing gates only. No real scientific evidence, rightsholder or legal permission was invented.
- Full impact/identity preview evaluates current archives and consumers for correctness. Large archives or many reviewed scopes can make detail/graph evaluation expensive; the bibliography list avoids that work. No production-scale latency claim is made.
- Browser evidence is Chromium on desktop and 320px viewport with keyboard/overflow checks. Local HTTP does not prove HTTPS/proxy/cookie deployment, physical mobile devices, screen-reader usability or actual legal/scientific review. External CI/deployment and independent review were not completed in this session.
